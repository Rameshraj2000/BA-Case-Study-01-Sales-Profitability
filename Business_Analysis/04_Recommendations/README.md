# Business Recommendations

This section documents the recommendations developed from the sales, profitability, discount and product-level analysis.

## Recommendation Areas

### Regional Performance
Actions based on differences in profitability and contribution across regions.

### Discount Management
Actions to reduce excessive discount exposure and protect profit margins.

### Product and Category Performance
Actions focused on loss-making products, sub-categories and categories with weaker profitability.

### Management Monitoring
Recommended KPIs and dashboard monitoring to support ongoing decision-making.

## Evidence

The detailed recommendations and executive business summary are included in the supporting documents in this section.

The recommendations are based on the findings identified through SQL analysis and the Power BI dashboard.
