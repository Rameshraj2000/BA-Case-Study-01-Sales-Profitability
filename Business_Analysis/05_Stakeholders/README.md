# Stakeholder Management & Communication

This section documents the stakeholders identified for the analysis and the communication approach used to support decision-making.

## Stakeholder Areas

### Business Management
Focus on overall sales performance, profitability and key business risks.

### Regional / Category Stakeholders
Focus on regional performance, category profitability and discount exposure.

### BI / Analytics
Focus on KPI definitions, dashboard requirements, data quality and ongoing monitoring.

## Communication Approach

The stakeholder communication plan defines the information required by each stakeholder group, the appropriate communication approach and the expected frequency of updates.
