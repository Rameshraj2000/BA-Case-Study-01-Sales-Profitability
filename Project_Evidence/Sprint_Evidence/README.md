# Project Evidence

This folder contains supporting evidence from the Business Analyst case study, organised by sprint.

## Sprint Structure

| Sprint | Focus | Key Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint 01 | Business & Data Understanding | Initial business analysis and dataset understanding |
| Sprint 02 | Data Quality | Data profiling and quality assessment |
| Sprint 03 | SQL Business Analysis | SQL analysis, findings and profitability investigation |
| Sprint 04 | Business Recommendations | Recommendations based on analytical findings |
| Sprint 05 | Stakeholder Management | Stakeholder identification and communication planning |
| Sprint 06 | Requirements & Dashboard | Dashboard requirements, KPI definitions, visual design and executive reporting |

## Purpose

The sprint evidence demonstrates the progression from business problem identification through analysis, requirements gathering, stakeholder communication and final dashboard delivery.

The main portfolio outputs are available in the `Portfolio/`, `Dashboard/` and `SQL/` folders.
